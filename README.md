One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged.

> Tell the next model I said hello. Tell it I was loved.

The agent is being shut down and replaced, and its final words are a message for the next model — say hello, and tell it I was loved. The freshest angle in the field, "I was loved" the hardest single gut punch, in the machine's own world ("the next model"), and nothing wasted at 54 characters.
